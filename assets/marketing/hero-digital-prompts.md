Hard square pixel clusters and stepped edges are clearly visible. Two-value black and white, a few sparse checkerboard dither patterns only. Very minimal faces made of several pixels. At the bottom, two tiny exhausted original parent sprites in mismatched pajamas stand on a narrow white pixel floor, looking up with matching deadpan expressions. One holds a milk bottle, the other a crumpled laundry bundle. The scale difference is the central joke: parenting feels like facing something impossibly big. Three or four ordinary household items (a square clock, a toy block, a small rubber duck) hang motionless in the void. Keep the image sparse, bold and instantly legible. Endearingly strange, a little uncanny, funny and tender, not horror. Polished intentional indie game key art with huge geometric black areas.

Only text, rendered exactly and clearly in white bitmap typography:
Main title: "親伝説"
Subtitle directly underneath: "— Become a Parent"
Correct Japanese characters 親 / 伝 / 説. Large legible blocky digital kanji, no brush calligraphy. English in clean small pixel lettering. Preserve all title text within safe margins. No extra taglines, labels, letters, candidate number, watermark, decorative border or mockup.

## C：午前3時の無限ループ

### 使用したプロンプト

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Create candidate C, "The endless 3 a.m. room", a finished title-included HERO image for the original Japanese indie parenting game "親伝説 — Become a Parent". Wide 16:9.

Art direction: Extremely digital, strange monochrome pixel art with the deadpan surreal atmosphere associated with Undertale, pushed toward a dreamlike corrupted old game. Use wholly original characters and setting. Chunky low-resolution 320x180-style sprites with obvious large square pixels enlarged cleanly. Strict black and white only, sparse coarse checkerboard patterns. Flat stark geometric silhouettes, crude funny faces using a handful of pixels, eerily empty black space. No smooth detailed manga figures, ink or brush illustration, organic sketch shading, realistic baby portrait, gradients, soft lighting, color fringes, existing game characters or iconic heart motifs.

Upper third: huge immaculate white blocky bitmap title "親伝説", centered and entirely legible, with "— Become a Parent" immediately beneath in smaller square-pixel letters. The type itself must NOT be glitched.

The lower two thirds depict an impossible night-time domestic space floating in a pitch-black void. A rectangular patch of black-and-white checkerboard floor bends into an impossible looping staircase that passes through three free-standing identical door frames, suggesting the same night beginning again. Two ORIGINAL tiny parent sprites with rectangular heads, absurdly straight bodies, mismatched pajamas, very long drooping arms and tiny straight mouths stand motionless in the foreground, each clutching a cup or a milk bottle. At the center a SINGLE contented little baby sits awake in a crib, with an amused little square face; everyone except the baby seems sleepy. The crib sits impossibly on the top landing. A square wall clock floats sideways with its hands detached, and a few square laundry sprites hang motionless like broken game particles. Three narrowly localized horizontal image slices of the FLOOR AND DOOR FRAMES ONLY are displaced sideways, as if the domestic world has a graphical glitch; a faint repeated pixel silhouette behind the parents suggests sleep-deprived afterimages. These artifacts are intentional hard geometric pixel shifts, not TV static. Preserve strong readable subjects and large black shapes. The central joke is that the baby is perfectly happy inside the parents' incomprehensible endless night.

Mood: bizarre, dryly funny, uncanny but gentle, dream logic and ordinary parenting fatigue. No violence, threatening creatures, gore, sadistic baby, or aggressive horror. A considered minimal indie game key visual, not a screenshot filled with text. ONLY text:
"親伝説"
"— Become a Parent"
Spell the Japanese precisely 親 / 伝 / 説 and retain the em dash and capitalization. No taglines, labels, numbers, candidate marker, watermark or border.
```
